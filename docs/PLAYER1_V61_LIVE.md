# Player 1 V61 live: integration record (build v61, 9 November 2026)

**Status: the V61 character is Player 1's live figure in this build; `efe.gltf.json` (V55) is the fallback and still draws everyone else on the hero rig.** Nothing deploys until the owner merges `claude/v61-live` into `master`.

The owner's request was "Make v61 the live version". This record says exactly what that means in the game, what was measured, and what the V59 and V61 reviews left open (unchanged by this build). Build-folder paths below are under `SP = /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Compound-Days/2b69ed99-9e54-5c05-ada2-7c1f5facd2b2/scratchpad` (the build notes, not the repository).

## 1. The asset: `dist/assets/characters/player1-v61.glb`

Converted by `tools/player1_v61_convert.py` from the owner's `Player1_V61_BLUE_FITTING_MOBILE.glb` (11,795,028 bytes, SHA-256 `c2950c3493fad3501513d214d7d67ee55356e080f02c1573b7d3814062991514`), the mobile variant of the V61 fitting pack (`docs/PLAYER1_V61_EVERYDAY_FIT.md`). Output: 5,101,400 bytes, SHA-256 `7b6548f0b0243d99e0389514cd930cb763bdff5c7b8d6eec01e74bc0ab21f62b`, glTF 2.0 binary.

What the conversion does:

- The 0.01-scale `Armature` root becomes the scale-1 root `Player1`; every bone translation and every animation translation track is multiplied by 0.01 (centimetres to metres); the inverse bind matrices are left-multiplied by S(0.01), so they are unit-rotation blocks with metre translations (Hips about [-0.006, -0.937, -0.016]). The skinned world positions equal the original's within 0.00001 m at Idle 0 s, Walk 0.5 s and Pick 1.25 s (a three.js r128 probe run during the build, `SP/v61/build/probe.cjs`, not in the repository).
- Dropped: the `skin_underlay` primitive (the pack's provisional shrinkwrapped under-body, 40,037 triangles), the `TANGENT` attribute, all five morph targets, the normal and metal-rough JPEGs, the `Run` clip.
- Kept: one mesh `Body` with six primitives, materials in order `skin` (16,115 triangles), `hair` (29,939), `top` (15,293), `bottom` (24,744), `shoe` (5,418), `top` (145, the untextured underarm patch, colour (0.32, 0.49, 0.77)); 91,654 triangles; attributes `POSITION`, `NORMAL`, `TEXCOORD_0`, `JOINTS_0`, `WEIGHTS_0`; one skin with the 27 joints in hero order (names identical to V55); the owner's 1024 × 1024 albedo JPEG (238,284 bytes, byte-identical to the pack's); eight clips `Idle` 0.03 s (a still pose), `Walk` 1.07, `Carry` 2.20 (still), `CarryLow` 2.20 (still), `WalkCarry` 1.03, `WalkCarryLow` 1.03, `Pick` 2.50, `Wave` 2.00; root extras `headCentre [-0.0019, 1.5452, 0.0555]`, `hairMount {x:0, y:1.5452, z:0, scale:0.43, headR:0.3}`, `version 61`, `visualRevision 'v61'`, `source`, `sourceSha256`.
- Known quirk: `hairMount.y` is the world-space head centre (equal to `headCentre[1]`), not the Head-joint-space value `CHARACTER.md` asks for (`efe`: 0.2734), because `tools/player1_v61_convert.py:101` multiplies the Head translation by zero. Nothing on the V61 figure reads it (the game's hair mount is removed and kit pieces are not mounted, section 2); the asset is frozen for this build, so the converter is left as shipped and both must change together if kit head items are ever to sit on V61.
- Measured in r128: bounding sphere centre (0, 0.85, 0), radius 1.02 m (the source's was 1 cm at the feet, which culled the figure in close-ups); 6 draw calls; heights Idle 0 to 1.697 m, Walk 0.014 to 1.694 m, Pick 0 to 1.583 m.

## 2. Runtime behaviour: `dist/src/hero-v61.js`

A classic script loaded after `head-v55.js` (the last module tag in `dist/index.html`), wrapping the base functions and always calling the originals. Its state is the global `HERO61`: `url`, `state` (idle, loading, ready, error), `gltf`, `clips`, `error`, `startedAt`, `loadedMs`, `bytes`, `enabled`, `building`, `albedo`, `patchColor`, `sourceColors` (each textured material's authored colour by name), `faceCam`, `note` (the creator line) and `kitNote` (the kit-panel line).

- **Who is Player 1.** `hero61Applies(look)` is true for the look with `pi` 0 alone (and `hero61 !== false`). `visual-v21` gives both players `rig:'hero'` and builds the named neighbours, the sellers and the football kids on the hero rig too, so all of them stay the classic hero `efe.gltf.json` with its wardrobe. In two-phone play `S` is the shared household and `players[0]` is always built from `S.p[0]`: Player 1 is household slot 0, the host's character, drawn with V61 on both phones; the guest's own figure (slot 1) is the classic hero. `hero61Drawn()` says whether the Player 1 standing in the world is the V61 figure.
- **Switch.** `hero61Switch(search, stored)`: `?hero=v61` forces the file on, `?hero=v55` or `localStorage['cd3-hero']==='v55'` turns it off, anything else leaves it on; the result is `HERO61.enabled`. `HERO.url` is never changed, so `check/runtime-harness.cjs` still loads the efe file.
- **Loading.** `heroLoad` is wrapped: when enabled it requests `assets/characters/player1-v61.glb?v=BUILD_ID`, then always calls the original, which requests `efe.gltf.json` through authored-v34's wrapper (that wrapper installs authored-v34's passes; the module never calls `authored34Install` itself, and the harness checks `AUTHORED34.installed` from a fresh state). On the V61 load it validates like `heroLoad` (bones through `heroFindBones`, a skinned mesh, idle and walk clips through `heroClipName`, an albedo), records the albedo, each textured material's colour and the patch colour, marks the scene `userData.hero61`, records `loadedMs` and `bytes`, then rebuilds Player 1 (`refreshAvatar(0)`) and Player 1's creator preview (`pv3d0`) only (`hero61Refresh`). On any error (network, parse, validation) `hero61Fail` sets `state 'error'` with a readable reason (GLTFLoader hands a `ProgressEvent` for a failed download, which reads `network error`), warns `[Compound Days] V61 file for Player 1 not used; the classic asset stands in:` with the reason, and rebuilds Player 1 from the classic hero.
- **Building Player 1.** `makeHeroAvatar` is wrapped: for Player 1's look with V61 ready it swaps `HERO.gltf/state/clips` for the V61 data while the original runs (restored in `finally`), then `hero61Dress`: the textured materials get the albedo in the asset's own colours (white for skin, hair, bottom and shoe; the tee's blue for `top`) with vertex colours off; the untextured `top` patch keeps that blue; `skin` materials carry `userData.authored34Skin` so authored-v34's 0.78 shade is not applied; every mesh is visible (the original hides the `hair` primitive through `heroIsHairMesh`); any `hairMount` or `wardrobeHair` child is removed; `g.userData.hero61`, `g.hero.v61` and an empty `g.hero.wardrobe` are set; `frustumCulled` stays off. If the base builder or the dressing throws, `HERO61.state` becomes `'error'` with the reason, the console warns `[Compound Days] V61 figure for Player 1 could not be built; the classic asset stands in:`, and the same call returns the classic figure for Player 1 (the look passed with `hero61:false`), so a V61 fault never marks the classic asset broken (heroLoad's own refresh runs inside the try that validates `efe.gltf.json`). `wardrobeEquip` is wrapped to return an empty wardrobe while a V61 avatar builds (the authored garments were built on the V55 bind pose and would float). `makeAvatar` is wrapped outside kit-v50's wrapper and passes Player 1's look with `kit` removed, so no `kit50_*` mount is built on the figure and its own `shoe` primitive stays visible; `S.p[0].kit` is untouched.
- **Creator.** `creator30Preview` is wrapped after head-v55's: in the face view of a V61 preview the camera comes from `HERO61.faceCam` (y 1.585, z 0.84, look 1.56), tuned so the smaller head's hair top and eyes land where the classic face view puts them (eyes at about 1.57 m, crown 1.70 m, chin about 1.44 m). `thumbRender` is wrapped so Player 1's hair, headwear and outfit thumbnails render the option on the classic hero with the chosen piece (the look is passed with `hero61:false`; `rig` stays `'hero'`), as before v61, because the V61 figure would show the same picture for every option. `openPanel` is wrapped to insert one line (class `v61note`) into Player 1's creator panel after the Skin tone field and before the Hair field, which puts it under polish-v30's tab row with the choices it speaks of (polish-v30 inserts the tabs before the first field): "Player 1 now uses the new V61 character, which wears its own look. The skin tone, hair, headwear and outfit chosen here colour Player 1’s portraits, not this figure." The same wrapper inserts one line into the kit panel (kit-v50) while Player 1 is the selected wearer and is drawn with V61: "Player 1 is drawn as the V61 figure, which shows no kit pieces; a piece Player 1 puts on is recorded here and drawn only on the classic hero (?hero=v55)." Neither line is inserted twice into one panel.
- **Menu.** `heroStatusText` is wrapped. Ready: `Character asset: assets/characters/player1-v61.glb loaded in N ms (8 clips). Player 1 wears the V61 look; Player 1’s skin tone, hair, headwear and outfit choices colour the portraits, not the V61 figure, and kit pieces are not drawn on it.` then `Classic asset: assets/characters/efe.gltf.json loaded in N ms (8 clips).` with Player 2's hair and clothes (visual-v21's `Player 1: modelled body; hair fallback; clothes base slots.` fragment is dropped by the `heroHairLine` wrapper). Loading: `Character asset: loading assets/characters/player1-v61.glb…` then the classic line. If the classic file failed while V61 is in use: `The classic asset failed as well (reason); Player 2 and the neighbours use the game’s built-in figure.` (never "Player 1 is drawn with the built-in avatar", which would be false). After a V61 failure: `V61 file not used (reason). Player 1 uses the classic hero.` and then the classic line (the middle sentence only once the classic file is ready). Switched off: the classic line unchanged. Every new line was checked against the shipped text for the four-word rule (no five consecutive words in common; `SP/v61/wf-build/fixer/overlap-fixer.json`).
- No `Math.random`, no `Date.now` (only `performance.now()` for the load time, as `heroLoad` does); every new name carries `61`.

## 5. Measured cost versus V55

Headless Chromium draws with SwiftShader, a software rasterizer, on a shared four-core machine: these numbers compare with each other only, not with a phone. Whole-frame values are `renderer.info.render.calls/triangles` after a frame of the game's own `tick()`, and the median of 60 consecutive `requestAnimationFrame` intervals, with both players standing in the courtyard just after moving in (idle) and with Player 1 walking right (D held). `render ms` times `renderer.render` alone. Both variants were measured by the tester in one run with the same setup script, V55 through `?hero=v55` (`SP/v61/wf-build/tester/raw/cost.json`; loads from `v61-desk.json`, `v61-phone.json`, `v55-desk.json`).

Draw calls and triangles are lower with V61 in every view; the frame medians are mixed (lower walking at 1280 × 800, higher elsewhere) and are set by the software rasterizer, not by the character (`render ms` is 6 to 8 ms in every cell). Load times vary from 2 to 13 s run to run on this shared machine. The V61 asset is 817,003 bytes smaller than `efe.gltf.json`; Player 1's group draws 8 meshes instead of 26, and its 91,654 triangles are 9,694 fewer than the V55 hero's 101,348. Both files download at boot (lead decision D1), since the classic hero still draws Player 2 and the neighbours.

## 6. Known since the V59 and V61 reviews, and what this build adds to the list

From `docs/PLAYER1_V59_FACIAL_AND_WARDROBE_AUDIT.md`, `docs/PLAYER1_V60_COOP_ACTIONS.md` and `docs/PLAYER1_V61_EVERYDAY_FIT.md`, unchanged by this build:

- **Face.** The Meshy face has no real lid or mouth topology; the source's five experimental morph targets (the V59 audit: mild lid changes only, no real closure or oral cavity) are stripped here, so Player 1's face is static (no blink, no wave smile). The nose and neck-seam artefacts the V58/V59 audits describe are in the texture and geometry as the owner delivered them.
- **Hair.** The reviews' open hair gate is topology, not motion: the hair is baked into the figure as one skinned primitive and cannot be separated or swapped for the game's hairstyles (V59 audit, V60 remaining work), and the V61 fit notes call its topology incomplete. Hair moving through the body or clothes was not measured here.
- **Wardrobe and kit.** The bind pose is Meshy's, not the hero's: the sixteen authored pieces do not fit and are not bound. The creator's skin, hair, headwear and outfit choices, the painted outfits, aso-ebi and kit pieces do not change Player 1's figure (the skin tone, hair, headwear and outfit still drive Player 1's 2D portraits). Kit needs V61 head and foot anchors (and a Head-joint-space `hairMount` in the asset) before it can sit on the figure. The pack's provisional under-body is not shipped.
- **Phone QA.** 91,654 triangles is not a phone-optimised count and no real iPhone or Android measurement exists; the costs above are software-rendered. `Pick` is a reach toward a low surface, not a floor pickup; the carry clips hold no prop.

Seen in this build's captures and measurements (visible limits, none fixed here):

- The idle is a 0.03 s still (arms held about 25 to 30° from the body, no motion) and `Carry`/`CarryLow` are stills; the separated garments have triangle-level (sawtooth) edges, visible at the collar in the phone face view (`v61-phone-creator-face.png`).
- The tee renders pale and desaturated under the game's lights (creator tee chest about (198, 208, 216), world (176, 192, 206), against albedo × factor ≈ (77, 113, 172)); skin renders brighter than its albedo. The preview lights (`dist/index.html:5303`) were tuned for the dark painted V55 colours. Left as the owner's call: a V61-only factor on `top` in `hero61Dress` would be the place.
- The sofa sit uses polish-v30's v21 constants (foot joint pinned 0.10 m above the floor); V61's Foot joint rides about 0.03 m higher above its sole than the classic's, so its soles may sink about 3 cm into the floor when seated (not visible at capture scale: `tester/raw/v61-desk-world-sit.png`, `fixer/raw/v61-desk-hero-sit.png`).
- The figure is shorter (crown 1.70 m against the V55 hair top of about 1.88 to 1.95 m), so polish-v30's body view leaves more headroom above V61 (desk canvas hair-top row 134 against 99 for V55), and the face view, which matches V55's hair-top and eye rows, fills about 16% less of the canvas width (figure 466 of 698 px against 552). Cosmetic; accepted.
- At 1280 × 800 the creator preview canvas (698 × 873 CSS px) is taller than the viewport, so the face view's eyes sit below the fold and the sticky button overlays the lower canvas; identical with `?hero=v55`, so pre-existing layout.
- Boot rebuilds the V61 Player 1 several times (the V61 arrival, heroLoad's own refresh when efe arrives, and once per wardrobe piece in Player 1's look as it is prefetched, although V61 ignores the wardrobe); each build clones the 91,654-triangle figure and allocates 8 materials, and `refreshAvatar` never disposes the old ones (pre-existing). A known cost, not changed here.
- An improvement, recorded: V61 walks with less foot slide (slide ratio median 0.12 to 0.14 against 0.33 to 0.40 for V55) and never puts a vertex below the floor (lowest vertex while walking 0.014 m against -0.029 m for V55; `tester/analysis.json`).

## 7. Provenance

The model is the owner's (Meshy-generated, fitted and split in the owner's V56 to V61 passes); the conversion tool and the module are ours. `docs/PROVENANCE.md`, v61, names the source file and hash. Nothing from MakeHuman, `efe.gltf.json` or the wardrobe files is in the asset.

## 8. Switching, for players and testers

- `?hero=v55` in the address, or `localStorage.setItem('cd3-hero','v55')`, loads the classic hero for Player 1; `?hero=v61` forces V61; nothing else is stored. Player 2 and the neighbours are the classic hero either way.
- In two-phone play "Player 1" is household slot 0, the host's character, on both phones: the guest sees the host as V61 and their own figure as the classic hero. That is the code's intent, not a defect.
- The Menu's status line says which asset draws Player 1 and, on a failure, why the classic one stands in; the kit panel says, while Player 1 is selected, that kit pieces are not drawn on the V61 figure.
