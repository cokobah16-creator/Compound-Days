COMPOUND DAYS — WATERSIDE
Build: v41-02b2e4da (authored character, your day in Palm Court, a bench for evening gist, monkey post, evening gist and daily finds, Aunty Bisi and thanks at the rail, notices and fresh evenings, Ada's commissions)
shown in the game Menu and recorded in PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json.

START HERE
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open a terminal inside the Compound_Days folder.
3. Run: python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory dist
   On Windows, use: py -m http.server 8000 --directory dist
4. Open http://localhost:8000 in your browser.

Use an HTTP server; opening index.html directly is not supported.
No installation or build is needed to play. Python is only one way to serve the files.

Choose a companion or two players sharing one screen. New households begin the
first-evening guide. Existing households can opt in through Tasks.
WASD + E for Player 1; arrow keys + Enter for Player 2; touch controls on phones.

CONTENTS
- dist/: playable game, JavaScript, styles, models, textures and vendor libraries.
- dist/assets/characters/efe.gltf.json: the authored character; dist/assets/wardrobe/: its 16 pieces.
- tools/: wardrobe, hair and material authoring tools, the build stamp, the manifest tool and
  the testers' tools (gates-v35.cjs, quiet-v38.cjs) and the reader's-sheet tools (reader-v38.cjs,
  reader-v39.cjs, reader-v40.cjs, reader-v41.cjs).
- check/: the Node verification harness: twenty harnesses (verify-*.cjs) and their results
  (results-*.json); README.md, section Verification, lists them and what a passing run prints.
- docs/: the playtest guides (TESTING_bench.md, TESTING_football.md, TESTING_gist.md,
  TESTING_v35.md), the Nigerian reader's sheets (READER_football.md, READER_gist.md),
  PROVENANCE.md and earlier plans.
- README.md: features, what this build keeps and removes, run and verification instructions.
- CHARACTER.md: the character and wardrobe binding contract and how to deliver a new base mesh.
- CONTRIBUTING.md: repository layout, imports and the build stamp.
- PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json: build id and SHA-256 hashes of packaged files.

Saves belong to the browser and origin where you play. The live Site's save does
not automatically move to localhost. Saves from builds v26-v41 load; their retired
outfit and hair choices are mapped to the authored wardrobe. Companion and
same-screen play are supported; cross-device online play is not provided by this
static build.
